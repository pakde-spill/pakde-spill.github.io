1. Personal-use utility

The OAuth client is operated only by the Pakde Spill project owner for private project backup, verification, and restore. It is not a public software service, storage provider, or account-management product.

2. No public account or service

This website does not offer registration, authentication, file upload, customer support accounts, purchases, or access to project data. Its purpose is limited to public app identity and policy disclosure.

3. Owner responsibility

The owner is responsible for safeguarding local credentials, reviewing backup logs, maintaining sufficient Google Drive storage, testing restores, and revoking access if a device or credential is compromised.

4. Availability

The utility and information site may be changed, paused, or discontinued. Backup integrity depends on successful transfer and verification; an untested backup should not be treated as guaranteed recovery.

Google Drive, Google OAuth, GitHub, GitHub Pages, and rclone are governed by their own terms and policies. Pakde Spill does not control the availability or policy changes of those services.
